Hi, I'm Zhengjie. I'm a dual-degree undergraduate studying computer science at the University of Michigan and mechanical engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

I'm interested in theoretical computer science, especially graph algorithms, parameterized algorithms, combinatorial optimization, and matroids. I'm also interested in computational complexity and cryptography.

## Research and projects

**Connectivity oracles under edge failures.** Exploring graph connectivity under edge failures in research discussions with Thatchaphol Saranurak. A write-up is available upon request.

**Metric minimum-cost perfect matching.** Investigating approximation approaches and structural questions in research discussions with Seth Pettie.

**Randomized algorithms for k-Path.** Studied random-walk algorithms for k-Path in a parameterized algorithms course taught by Euiwoong Lee.

**Robust pseudorandom generation.** Investigated robustness and privacy questions for pseudorandom generators in advanced cryptography.
